## Review Focus

- A first scoring card debuffed by a boss: no repeats, no Lucky/Glass draws; Chad does not transfer to the next active card (Task2).
- Repeated Lucky payout crosses Bull/Bootstraps and cashout-interest thresholds: each roll/payout is independent and committed exactly once (Task2).
- Heart changes the disabled Joker after play, or Bell draws after Glass: replay uses score-time Joker/counter/RNG history rather than current flags (Task2).
