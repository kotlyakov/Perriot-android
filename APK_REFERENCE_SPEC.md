## Shop — confirmed directly from APK

### Initial state
Source: assets/game.lua, GAME initialization.
- `base_reroll_cost = 5`.
- `current_round.reroll_cost = 5`.
- `shop.joker_max = 2`.
- Rates: `joker_rate=20`, `tarot_rate=4`, `planet_rate=4`, `spectral_rate=0`, `playing_card_rate=0`.
- `discount_percent=0`, `interest_cap=25`, `interest_amount=1`.

### Shop layout/content
Source: assets/game.lua `Game:update_shop`; assets/functions/UI_definitions.lua `G.UIDEF.shop`.
- Main shop area fills to `G.GAME.shop.joker_max` cards (default 2).
- Voucher area presents the current round voucher if valid.
- Booster area has two positions.
- Shop has Next Round and Reroll controls.
- Existing shop state is loaded rather than regenerated when restoring a run.

### Main shop card generation
Source: assets/functions/UI_definitions.lua `create_card_for_shop(area)`.
Default weighted type rates:
- Joker: 20
- Tarot: 4
- Planet: 4
- Playing card: 0 by default
- Spectral: 0 by default

The poll is seeded using `pseudorandom(pseudoseed('cdt'..ante))`; exact downstream `create_card` pool behavior must be mapped before claiming RNG parity.
Tags may override creation/modification before normal generation.

### Booster packs
Source: assets/functions/common_events.lua `get_pack` and assets/game.lua booster catalog/update_shop.
- Two booster slots are generated for a fresh shop.
- The first shop booster is forced to `p_buffoon_normal_1` or `_2` unless banned (`first_shop_buffoon`).
- Later packs are weighted from the Booster pool and seeded by key + ante.
- Normal/Jumbo/Mega variants carry their own cost, weight, `extra` and `choose` parameters in the catalog.

### Reroll
Source: assets/functions/button_callbacks.lua `can_reroll`, `reroll_shop`; assets/functions/common_events.lua `calculate_reroll_cost`.
- Reroll is unavailable when `(dollars - bankrupt_at) - reroll_cost < 0`, except a zero-cost reroll.
- Paid reroll subtracts current reroll cost.
- `free_rerolls` is consumed separately.
- Each reroll clears only the main shop-card area and refills it to `joker_max`; voucher and booster areas are not regenerated by this function.
- Normal reroll increments `reroll_cost_increase` by 1, therefore default sequence is $5, $6, $7... .
- If free rerolls remain, displayed reroll cost is 0.
- Joker effects receive `reroll_shop` context after the reroll.
- Run is saved after reroll.

### Persistence/lifecycle
Source: assets/game.lua `Game:update_shop`.
- Restored `shop_jokers`, `shop_vouchers`, and `shop_booster` are loaded instead of regenerated.
- A fresh shop generates missing content then saves the run.
- Tags receive `shop_start`, `voucher_add`, `shop_final_pass`, and shop-card creation/modification contexts at defined points.

## Shop economy and transitions — confirmed directly from APK

### Affordability / bankruptcy floor
Source: `assets/functions/button_callbacks.lua` `can_buy`, `can_buy_and_use`, `can_redeem`, `can_open`, `can_reroll`.
- A purchase is affordable when its cost does not take available dollars below `bankrupt_at`; zero-cost items are allowed.
- Reroll uses the same `dollars - bankrupt_at` floor.
- Voucher and booster affordance uses the same floor.
- Slot availability is checked separately from affordability for normal shop cards.

### Card purchase price
Source: `assets/card.lua` `Card:set_cost`.
1. `extra_cost` starts at current run inflation.
2. Edition surcharge: Foil +2, Holographic +3, Polychrome +5, Negative +5.
3. Pre-discount amount is `base_cost + extra_cost`.
4. Price is `max(1, floor((base_cost + extra_cost + 0.5) * (100-discount_percent)/100))`.
5. Booster ante-scaling modifier adds `ante - 1` after that calculation.
6. Astronomer makes Planet cards and Celestial boosters free.
7. Rental cards cost 1.
8. Couponed cards in main shop or booster area cost 0.
Tutorial-only price modification exists and must not be applied to normal runs.

### Sell value / selling
Source: `assets/card.lua` `Card:set_cost`, `Card:sell_card`, `Card:can_sell_card`.
- Base sell value is `max(1, floor(cost/2)) + extra_value`.
- Selling credits `sell_cost`, then dissolves/removes the card.
- Only cards in a joker-type owned area are sellable by this path, and Eternal cards cannot be sold.
- Selling invokes the selling card's `selling_self` context and other jokers receive `selling_card` context via callback.
- Special blind/effect interactions can occur on sale; they must remain event-driven rather than folded into the money calculation.

### Buying a normal shop card
Source: `assets/functions/button_callbacks.lua` `check_for_buy_space`, `buy_from_shop`.
- Capacity is checked independently for Jokers and consumables; Negative edition can add one effective slot for that card.
- Purchased playing cards enter the deck and `playing_cards` list.
- Purchased consumables enter the consumable area; Jokers enter the joker area.
- Buying triggers `buying_card` contexts.
- Money is charged exactly once after the transfer path is accepted.
- If inflation modifier is active, inflation increments after a purchase and all card costs are recalculated.
- Buy-and-use consumables use the same purchase path, then immediately invoke use.

### Voucher redeem
Source: `assets/functions/button_callbacks.lua` use-card Voucher path; `assets/card.lua` `Card:redeem`.
- Redeeming a shop voucher marks it used, clears the current-round voucher, charges its cost, applies the voucher to the run, and triggers buying-card contexts.
- Inflation, if active, increments and recalculates prices after redeem.

### Booster open
Source: `assets/functions/button_callbacks.lua` use-card Booster path; `assets/card.lua` booster open path.
- Opening a purchased booster marks its `used_packs[booster_pos]` entry as `USED`, preventing regeneration of that slot on shop restoration.
- Pack content/choice behavior is a separate subsystem and remains to be fully traced before implementation.

### Reroll price state machine
Source: `assets/functions/button_callbacks.lua` `reroll_shop`; `assets/functions/common_events.lua` `calculate_reroll_cost`.
- Paid reroll charges the current displayed cost.
- `final_free` is captured before decrementing `free_rerolls`.
- `calculate_reroll_cost(final_free)` means consuming a free reroll does NOT increment `reroll_cost_increase`.
- A normal paid reroll increments `reroll_cost_increase` by 1 before computing the next displayed cost.
- Therefore with default base 5 and no modifiers: first display 5; after paying 5 -> 6; after paying 6 -> 7. A free reroll displays 0 and, when consumed, returns to the current paid tier rather than advancing it.

### Leaving shop
Source: `assets/functions/button_callbacks.lua` `toggle_shop`.
- Before leaving, every owned Joker receives `ending_shop` context.
- Shop UI/state is removed and game state changes to `BLIND_SELECT`.
- This transition does not itself select/start a blind; blind selection is a separate action.
